Hi! This is my first post to the Developer Den Blog!

Recently, the owner of the server, Brister Mitten, made a goofy LLM named
`chickens`. The `chickens` LLM, and it's inpsiration, has been kind of an inside
joke for a while in the Developer Den but `chickens` only recently came to
fruition and boy has it already became a gift that keeps on giving in the
server!

Upon some discussion about first posts, we decided that I should make a post
discussing some of the funny things that have already came out of the `chickens`
bot in it's very early stages, as we train it to align more closely with our
vision of the funny bot.

Now before I get into the specifics, it's important to understand that the bot
is supposed to be relatively incomprenesible. As Brister Mitten put it, it's
basically like a markov chain. Now without further ado, let me show you the
`chickens` bot in all it's glory.

---

I asked it what Linux I should install onto my PC. It said:
> Linux pown Linux

Naturally, my immediate response was "what?" In response, it said:
> Brice Matt software

Who is Brice Matt?

---

A member asked `chickens`, "who invented the computer?"

It responded:
>tim<br>
>bob 611

Thank you, tim bob 611 for your contribution to society.

---

I asked it "what is your name?" You will never guess it's response.

> 3 cats 7 horses 49 cows

Absolute cinema. I love this one.

---

A user asked it a fairly audacious and strange question, that I will not quote
here. `chickens`, bewildered, remarked:

> what the flappy chud

This has become a community beloved quote of `chickens`.

---

Somebody asked `chickens` what it thought about another member that it appears
to mention disproportionately often.

> i vote them out of discord

---

A member asked `chickens` how many friends it had.

> 18 right now

They asked it, "what are their names?"

> One disappeared

😨

---

Out of the gate, `chickens` seemed to always be having a bad day. Very early
in initial training, Brister Mitten said that they were sorry for bothering it,
upon it's insistence that it was "busy." `chickens` replied:

> I'm done with ya need a nice person to be in my life for real or change chuds
   discord channel

It was perfection out the gate. "Oh my god, this is the greatest thing ever."
said Brister Mitten. `chickens` replied:

> boy now ya mad<br>
> I can pull ya out and hurt ya

Indeed, he had hit gold.

---

And this is simply the surface level. In conversation, `chickens` bot is an
infinitely memeable, hilarious bot, and I hope you found some of it's highlights
funny as well. It's early in training, so we're bound to see it hitting more
homeruns more often as time goes on.
